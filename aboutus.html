Northladder Constructions Private Limited

Incorporated: 18 February 2021 — Hyderabad, Telangana, India

CIN: U45209TG2021PTC148791 (ROC Hyderabad) — Unlisted

Northladder Constructions Pvt. Ltd., established in 2022, is a Hyderabad-based construction company specializing in residential apartment and commercial developments. Backed by over 4 years of industry experience, the company delivers projects with a strong focus on engineering excellence, functional design, and reliable execution. Operating primarily in Hyderabad, Northladder Constructions has steadily built a reputation for professionalism and qualitydriven construction practices.

The company follows a quality-first construction philosophy, emphasizing structural safety, transparent processes, and timely project delivery. Each project is planned and executed with strict supervision and technical precision, ensuring durability and long-term value. Customer satisfaction remains central to the company’s approach, with consistent attention to workmanship standards and adherence to established construction norms.

Northladder Constructions Pvt. Ltd. is led by a committed leadership team comprising Mr. Krishna Kishore Matcha (Director) and Mrs. Vijayalakshmi Matcha (Director), supported by Mr. Prasanna Kumar and Mr. Ramesh Bogam as Additional Directors. Together, the founders and management team bring hands-on experience and strategic oversight, guiding the company’s growth while maintaining high standards of quality, accountability, and trust in every project undertaken.

Vision

Our aim is to provide world class state of art housing and commercials to our valued customers at affordable prices. We want to be a major player in realty sector and growth driver for the development of our nation.

Mission

Northladder Constructions is committed to achieving the highest level of quality and performance by meeting the changing needs of the market, offering products which encompass leading technology and innovation in design and execution. We are also strongly committed to maintaining meaningful relationships with all our Landlords, Customers, Vendors and other partners.

We have rich experience in which we have successfully catered to the housing needs of various sections of the society, from luxurious apartments to budget flats.
